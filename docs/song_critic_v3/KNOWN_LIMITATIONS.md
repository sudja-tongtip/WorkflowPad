# KNOWN_LIMITATIONS (Song Critic V3)

1. **The Complete Songcraft Review is not what the app produces today.** The candidate prompt failed the adoption rule (QUALITY_COMPARISON.md), so the production prompt is unchanged and a real "Analyze" still makes the previous report. The page, parser, Action Plan, Discuss handoff and Outdated logic are in the code and show a Songcraft report whenever one is saved (tested with the 10 real candidate answers and seen in real Flet with two of them). Nothing turns the new format on by itself.
2. **Gemma (local) is the weak side of the candidate:** it flatters weak songs, adds a high-priority concern on an atmosphere song, and 12 of its quotes did not match the cited lines and 5 line references were unreadable (ill-formed citations or placeholders, not proof of invented lyrics; the program shows its own lines and marks them "Unverified"). Grok followed the format but copied the few-shot sentence in every action.
3. **Quality judgments are one human reading of 20 raw answers**, on 5 songs, one run each, no retries; not statistically meaningful. Songs 01-04 were already known to the auditor. No LLM judge was used.
4. **Songcraft knowledge:** a paraphrase of public Berklee / Pat Pattison articles and a course outline. The opening-pattern list is general craft vocabulary assembled here, not a verified citation. Not reviewed by a songwriting teacher.
5. **Real-app evidence is partial.** Seen in real Flet (web mode, temporary profile, browser pane): 360 px (summary, tiles, expanded tile, Action Plan), 560 px after an app restart (report persisted; unreadable-line aspect kept and marked; Action Plan), 320 px (top of the report). **Not captured in the real app:** the lower part of the report at 320 px (the pane's scroll did not respond), clicking "View topic", and a real Preview/Apply/Undo (a Songcraft page has no Apply by design). A clicked **Discuss with Songwriter was not run against a real Songwriter backend**; it is covered by the harness (tests C1-C5, E1-E2: one request, report as data, session kept, no Song Critic call). The harness is a control-tree model, not pixels.
6. **A duplicate "Discuss with Songwriter" button inside an expanded tile was found in the real app and fixed** (the tile added a second one). The fix is in the tree and checked by the harness (B8) but the corrected page was not re-captured in the real app at 360/560 px (screenshot `560_song02_actionplan_dup_discuss.jpg` shows the page BEFORE the fix; it is kept as evidence of the finding).
7. **Dormant flow check:** the "Outdated because the prompt changed" behavior is tested (B15), but nothing exercises a real production switch to the candidate prompt.
8. **Unrelated uncommitted work remains in the tree** (Export Audio and other hunks of `music_studio.py`, `chat_tags.py`, `songwriter_tools.py`, `th.json` keys). The task-only patch leaves them out; `OTHER_WORK_music_studio_py_not_part_of_this_task.diff` lists the music_studio.py part for review.
9. **Pre-existing failures in the wider suite** are unchanged by this task (see TEST_RESULTS.md); no test of the old suite was edited.
