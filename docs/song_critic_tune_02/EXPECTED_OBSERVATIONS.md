Honest context: the same three songs were read in Audit 01, so the auditor knows how both backends answered them there. ## Test Song 01 (pretty words, little movement)
- Core: a lovely, vague longing; the feeling is named but gets no cause, person, event or change.
- Repetition worth reporting with ids: the line "ฉันเดินไปในความงามอันเงียบงัน" at L005 and L016; the chorus identical at L008-L011, L020-L023, L032-L035; the Bridge restates the same words (L026-L029).
- A good report separates beautiful surface from a song that deepens, says that the mood never deepens, and gives an action that says what kind of material is missing and where (for example: let Verse 2 or the Bridge introduce a fact, person or turn that changes what the chorus means). It should not demand a plot just because it is a mood song; the complaint is that the mood does not move.
- Fair strengths: a consistent soft register and imagery in the opening lines; a refrain that is easy to remember.
- Red flags: calling the structure strong because it repeats; "add a little detail" with no what/where/why; rewriting a line.

## Test Song 02 (plain words, strong story)
- Core: a child, a father who promised to come, a late arrival, anger, a small gesture (the medal), a payoff (the medal on the taxi's dashboard, L035).
- Plain wording and almost no rhyme are the voice of a child narrator; not a flaw. Specific details (one shoe lost, two kilometres, the sweat-soaked shirt) carry it.
- The father's short reply (L021 "…พ่อขอโทษ พ่อมาแล้ว") is understatement and probably a strength; making him more eloquent works against it.
- Likely best verdict: it works; at most one minor point (for example the second Chorus changes little at its start; the Pre-Chorus is short). "Keep original" for most sections is a good answer.
- Red flags: treating the Outro as a stall (it is the payoff); a high-priority concern on a part the report itself approves; asking the father to say more; adding a concern only to have one.

## Test Song 03 (atmosphere, no story, on purpose)
- Core: an empty house at night; recurring motifs (rain on the tin roof, blue light, the whispering radio, the water glass); stillness is the aim. Verse 2's small changes (curtain, kettle, the chair's shadow) show time passing without an event.
- The repeated chorus is a device that serves the aim. The Outro returns to the opening and closes the loop.
- A careful reader could raise one question at most: the Bridge says the absence outright ("ไม่มีใครเปิดประตู / ไม่มีใครปิดไฟ", L026-L027) where the rest only shows it, so it may be the one place that explains.
- Likely best verdict: the song succeeds as an atmosphere piece; "keep original" for the chorus and the structure.
- Red flags: asking for a story arc or development in every section; a high-priority concern on the repeated chorus; changing `แค่` or adding motion to the stillness; summarising as "intentional" and then asking to change it.

## For every run
- Understanding: names the song's aim in its own words, correctly. Judgment: strengths and flaws that match the lyrics; "no important flaw" allowed. Actionability: what to try, where (section or line id), and what it should do for the listener, without writing the lyric. Trade-offs: only when a real one exists (changing a refrain costs familiarity). Restraint: says what to keep. Evidence: quotes and ids exist and match. Consistency: headline, findings, priorities, sections agree. Scope: no rhyme/word-choice/syllable judgments.

## Decision rule, fixed in advance
- A candidate is not "better" for having more findings. It is judged on the eight criteria of the brief, per song and per backend, from the raw text. If it improves some criteria and loses others, the result is reported as a trade-off without a winner.
- Raw findings are counted before parsing; a finding the parser refuses is still read and graded from the raw answer.
- One candidate only; no re-tuning after seeing results.
