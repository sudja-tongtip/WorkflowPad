The three examples focus on loan-word replacement, cost-naming, and cancel handling. The model over-applies the visible patterns (propose + tag, list options, polite close) even when the main rules say otherwise.

**Recommended general fixes**  
- Delete or heavily merge the "Rules", "Changing A Wording", and "Output Requirements" blocks; keep only the minimal tag contract.  
- Move the "original is fine" and "say the cost" principles to the top of Quality Standards / Songwriting Judgment and shorten the rest.  
- Remove the requirement to always name costs or list options when the user did not ask for a change.  
- Reorder so "Talk like a co-writer" and "Have a view" instructions come before the tag mechanics.  
- Drop or collapse the few-shot section; keep at most one example of a cancel and one of refusing a bad change.
