## Decision (pre-registered, qualitative)
Condition: "must not lose the ability to criticise the weak song (S1) in either backend": **not met** (Gemma loses it in Pass 2; Grok softens it). On S3 B is at least as good. Cost is modest. Result: **mixed** (better on the atmosphere song, worse on the weak song) -> **keep production one-shot** and report the trade-off. The prototype is not put into production.

## Interpretation (not proven; two songs, one run each)
- The Pass 2 prompt ("keep conclusions that remain convincing, you do not need to change your opinion") plus a minimal system prompt with no stance on intention versus effect left the model free to choose, and it chose the comfortable reading: stillness was explained as the genre's intention. The earlier sequential test showed the same drift with more steps. Freedom + reconsideration did not by itself produce the courage the brief wants.
- The genre line in the song data ("Dream Pop, Wistful, Low energy") is a ready excuse, in both arms; it appeared in the reasons of Gemma and Grok B for S1.
- The minimal prompt contains no rule against writing lyrics or judging sound; Gemma did both (S1 arrangement advice, S3 replacement lines). Production's longer prompt contains those rules.
