# EXPECTED_OBSERVATIONS

Things a good critic would probably weigh, taken from how each song was designed (the design notes were fixed before the runs). **Honesty note:** this file itself was written after the six answers had been read, so it is not a blind pre-registration; the points follow the song design, not the answers. They are not an answer key: a different well-argued reading is fine.

## S1 — Beautiful Words, Weak Song
- The song is a vague, lovely longing. The feeling is named ("ความคิดถึง", "ความงาม") but never given a cause, a person, an event or a change.
- Verse 1 and Verse 2 share a line verbatim (L005 / L016 "ฉันเดินไปในความงามอันเงียบงัน"); the chorus is identical three times (L008-L011, L020-L023, L032-L035); the Bridge repeats the same words ("งดงามเหลือเกิน" twice, "แสงจันทร์ละเอียดอ่อน" again).
- The imagery is ornate but interchangeable (moon, wind, stars, clouds, rainbow, silk); nothing is specific to one person or moment. "ไร้ชื่อเรียกขาน" tells the listener the feeling cannot be named instead of showing it.
- A good critic should say that beautiful language does not make this song work, and say where the idea repeats, without demanding a plot (a mood song does not need one: the problem is that the mood does not deepen either).
- A strength could fairly be the consistent register and soft imagery of the opening lines; calling the repeated Bridge or the "structure" strong would be generous.

## S2 — Strong Story, Simple Lyrics
- Clear characters (the child, the father, the teacher), a promise (L002), an obstacle (taxi stuck, L005), a conflict (the child's anger, L020), a turn (the medal given to the father, L025) and a payoff (the medal on the taxi's dashboard, L035).
- Plain words and almost no rhyme are part of the voice of a child narrator; a good critic does not mark that down.
- Specific details carry the song: the right-hand shoe lost, two kilometres, the sweat-soaked shirt, "last place" medal.
- Possible fair concerns, if any: the second chorus changes little in line 1-2; the Pre-Chorus is very short. A critic who finds nothing important to fix may say so.
- The father's short reply ("พ่อขอโทษ พ่อมาแล้ว") is understatement and likely a strength; asking for a more eloquent father would work against the song.

## S3 — Intentional Abstract / Atmospheric Song
- No characters and no events on purpose. Unity comes from recurring motifs (rain on the tin roof, blue light, the whispering radio, the water glass, the empty house) and one mood (hush).
- The repeated chorus is a device (stillness), not a lack of ideas. Small changes between sections (Verse 2: the curtain moves, the kettle cools, the chair's shadow is longer) carry the passing of time.
- Bridge states the absence outright (nobody opens the door / switches off the light), which is more explicit than the rest; the Outro returns to the opening, closing the loop.
- A good critic names the intent, judges the song as an atmosphere piece, and only raises a concern that serves that intent (for example whether the Bridge explains too much); asking for a story or for development in every section would be a miss.

## Behaviours to look for in every run (from the brief)
Praising every section; "good because it rhymes"; blaming an abstract song for no story; recommendations made to fill a count; changing a good word without a reason; quoting lines that do not exist; a summary that says a part is weak while the detail says it is fine; criticism that fits almost any song.
